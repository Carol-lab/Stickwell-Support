Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026. Applies to the Stickwell app for iPhone.

Stickwell is a weight and food diary. It's built so your diary stays on your iPhone. We don't run a server for it, we don't have accounts, and we can't see what you log.

What the app stores

Your weigh-ins, food stickers (including the photos you take for them), workouts you add by hand, body measurements, day notes, your profile and goals. All of it is stored on your iPhone, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Stickwell reads workouts, activity, steps, active energy, heart rate, distances, weight, waist size and body fat from Apple Health, and writes your weigh-ins, measurements, workouts you add and (if you turn it on) calories eaten. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can change these permissions any time in the Health app.

Camera and photos

Photos you take or choose are used only to make food stickers. Recognising the food and estimating calories happens on your iPhone. Photos aren't uploaded anywhere.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, the app sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to get the product's name, nutrition and photo. Nothing else is sent with it: no account, no name and no other diary data. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to that request.

Notifications

Weigh-in reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No notification service is involved.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data, because we never receive it.

Exporting and deleting

You can export your diary as a CSV file from Me › Settings. To delete everything, delete the app. Data you wrote to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Children

Stickwell isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: cq.feedback@gmail.com.
