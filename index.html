Snap a meal, get a sticker

Stickwell lifts the food off your photo, gives it a die-cut border and estimates the calories. Packaged food? Scan the barcode.

Weigh in twice

Morning and night. Two numbers a day show the real trend, and a 7-day average smooths out water weight.

Every workout gets a Pip

Workouts from your Apple Watch show up by themselves, with Pip doing your sport. Active calories add to your budget.

A wrap-up every night

After your night weigh-in, the day folds into one card: calories, both weigh-ins and your plate as a collage.

A report every week

Weight change, average deficit, weigh-in streak, workouts, and roughly when you'll reach your goal.

Protein, carbs, fat

Macros for every meal, with targets set from your calorie budget. Plus a body archive for 13 measurements.

Meet Pip.

A lime pip who does every sport you do. Running, cycling, swimming, lifting, yoga, boxing, dancing and more.

Your diary stays
on your iPhone.

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • Calorie estimates are made on the device. No ads, no tracking.
  • Weigh-ins, workouts and measurements can sync with Apple Health, which you control.
  • Barcode lookups send only the barcode number to Open Food Facts.
  • Export everything as CSV whenever you like. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Something not working? Email cq.feedback@gmail.com and you'll hear back from a person.

How accurate are the calories?

They're estimates. Stickwell recognises the food on your iPhone and looks it up in a food table based on USDA data, then you confirm the name and portion. Packaged food uses the barcode's nutrition label from Open Food Facts.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Weigh-ins and food work on their own, and you can add workouts by hand. With an Apple Watch, workouts and active calories appear automatically.

Can I use pounds?

Not yet. Stickwell uses kilograms for now. Pounds are on the list.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, and in Apple Health if you turn that on. There's no Stickwell account or server. Deleting the app deletes the diary, so export a CSV first if you want to keep it.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
